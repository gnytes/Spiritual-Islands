Spiritual Islands Assessment

This assessment is not about finding yourself. It is about finding the ground when the storms of life surround you.

This framework meets you where you are. If you come from a tradition that teaches that God is the source of all meaning, this does not challenge that. It asks a different question: where does that relationship actually come alive in your lived experience?

If you aren't religious, this isn't asking you to become something you're not. It gives you language for what you already have, the ways you make sense of your life, what holds you steady, and where you go when things fall apart.
